# Learning + Handover Skill

## WHEN TO USE
Use when an AI Development Team agent discovers a reusable lesson, improved procedure, warning, correction, or more efficient method during execution.

## INPUT
Observed lesson, execution evidence, affected Masterplan item, affected skill/document, scope, limitations, and whether the lesson appears TeamAi-specific or generalizable.

## ACTION
1. Record what was discovered and why it matters.
2. Tie the lesson to the executed checklist/skill and evidence.
3. Put current continuation state in `docs/project-guide/HandOver.md`.
4. Request/record the applicable endorsement in `docs/project-guide/Endorsement.md`.
5. Update the appropriate operational skill when the procedure itself changed.
6. Update `AI_ASSISTANT_READ_ME.md` when the lesson is a practical session/recovery technique.
7. Update `PRODUCT-KNOWLEDGE.md` only when the lesson is validated and worth permanent retention.
8. Propose a ToolKit upstream candidate only after the lesson has enough evidence to test for generalization.

## DO NOT
- Promote an agent opinion directly to Product Law.
- Call a lesson generalized because it worked once.
- Store raw conversation or screenshot evidence in Product Knowledge.
- Change a skill without checking the governing Product Law and Policy.

## PASS
The lesson has an evidence path, a clear scope, an accepted destination, and a durable handover/endorsement record where required.

## EVIDENCE
Execution result, source/skill path, verification result, handover reference, endorsement state, and Product Knowledge row if promoted.
