## Structural prerequisite before C9

The current Seat hierarchy is a **partial working mechanism**, not the completed Hero machine. Before C9 can be accepted, the product must establish the required semantic tree/branch/division structure, branch-as-integration behavior, payload-driven geometry, expansion/adjacency/wiring space, meaningful tree-to-tree spatial travel, real connection topology, and the integrated turn-loop contribution layer. During the final turn-loop, participating divisions are active/open so their real wiring paths are spatially available.

The machine must support broad, asymmetric and recursively nested branches. Branch dimensions and positions are derived from the actual semantic/UI payload rather than copied prototype coordinates. Expansion is a smooth stateful machine transition, not an instant visibility change or teleport. Current timing values remain living implementation baselines, not final animation law.

The tree census is synchronized in four representations and must be reconciled by the same governed change whenever a tree/branch/division or associated semantic payload is added, removed, renamed, materially restructured, or reimplemented.

## C9 — Integrated product acceptance

C9 is the product acceptance gate after the structural prerequisites above and C8 are actually established. It requires current deployed evidence from the canonical public route, desktop and phone observation, and explicit owner/source-of-truth acceptance. Green CI, one browser test, one working Seat tree, a merged PR, or a non-canonical screenshot does not establish C9.
