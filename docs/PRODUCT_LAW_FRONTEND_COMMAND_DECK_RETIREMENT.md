# Frontend Product Surface Retirement — Command Deck

**Status:** NORMATIVE PRODUCT-LAW EXTENSION
**Parent:** `PRODUCT_LAW.md`
**Execution ledger:** Issue #278

The historical Command Deck is retired as a current TeamAi product and deployment surface.

The retirement removes a competing workspace shell, not the underlying capabilities. Future authenticated workspace functions are expressed through the spatial machine, semantic tree/branch/division surfaces, world-map Settings, and center workspace model.

`/spatial/` must not be published as a current product route. Historical `frontend/spatial/**` source and its prior contracts/tests/commits remain preserved as evidence and reconstruction material only.

Existing prototype animations, camera paths, ambient effects, and timing values remain replaceable implementation baselines. They must not constrain the future adaptive tree/branch/division expansion system or the semantic electrical turn-loop topology.

A later reactivation of the Command Deck concept requires an explicit Product Law decision and full reconciliation before implementation.
