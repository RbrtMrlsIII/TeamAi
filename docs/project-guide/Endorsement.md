# Endorsement — acceptance record

**Role:** record authorized acceptance of a precise completed scope or validated lesson. Not a roadmap, handover manual, policy, or authority source.

## Acceptance record

- **Scope:** record exact Product Law concept / Masterplan item / implementation surface.
- **Authority:** identify the approving human or governed source.
- **Evidence:** identify exact verification, environment, commit/PR, and limitations.
- **State:** `IMPLEMENTED | DEPLOYED | RUNTIME-PROVEN | LEARNED | COMPLETED | ENDORSED`.
- **Decision:** `ACCEPT | ACCEPT-WITH-LIMITATION | REJECT | SUPERSEDE`.

## Rules

A green test is evidence, not endorsement. Endorsement cannot create permission, authority, backend truth, entitlement, or release status. Reopening a meaningful architecture or canonical-root change requires fresh verification and a new acceptance record.

Learning accepted here may update the bounded Skill or `PRODUCT-KNOWLEDGE.md`. Product Law changes return to the Product Law authority path.
