### Structural rules

- `treeID` and `branchId` are semantic identities. Coordinates never define identity.
- A branch is a real integration with semantic purpose, responsibility, UI/product payload, expansion region, adjacency clearance, connection ownership, camera relationship, responsive/reduced-motion behavior, and verification evidence.
- Trees may have broad branches, nested sub-branches, and different depths.
- Geometry is payload-driven. Branch count, dimensions, spacing, radius, angular spread, and expansion footprint may differ per tree.
- Expansion space is architectural because it must reserve room for adjacent divisions, wiring corridors, camera travel, readability, and responsive behavior.
- Maximum Hero expansion is derived from the combined footprint of active divisions, not a universal global scale.
- During the final turn-loop/contribution state, participating tree/branch divisions are expected to be active/open so their intended wiring paths are spatially available.
- Final electrical flow must follow actual semantic connection topology, not disconnected decorative paths.
- Division opening/closing must be smooth and stateful, not an instantaneous visibility or coordinate swap.
- Existing hierarchy/camera timings remain living implementation baselines, not final animation law.

## Current Hero machine vision

The Hero is intended to behave as an expandable technological machine rather than a static 3D scene.

A selected tree/branch may cause its machine division to open and reveal its semantic product payload: relevant features, configuration surfaces, controls, and accessibility surfaces. The geometry of that division must adapt to its payload.

The machine therefore evolves as:

`semantic tree → payload → expansion volume → connection topology → geometry → camera → interaction → contribution visualization`

not:

`prototype coordinates → decorative mesh → inferred meaning`.

## Current turn-loop vision

The final turn-loop is a semantic electrical contribution flow:

`active WebAi turn → active tree/branch → participating divisions open/active → wiring paths become traversable → signal travels through connected trees/branches → workspace center`

The animation itself is not final. Existing turn-loop/camera timing behavior must not be presented as the final visual language.
