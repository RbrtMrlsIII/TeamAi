## 0. Purpose

PR #398 established the reviewed structural 029 baseline. It gave the project a cleaner renderer ownership model, semantic machine topology, adaptive geometry foundations, Seat/domain convergence, and verification boundaries.

It did not finish the intended 3D machine.

This document is the durable construction plan for the actual spatial world from the current skeleton through product-feature integration, spatial completion, visual refinement, production observation, and human acceptance.

The target is a coherent physical machine:

CENTRAL CORE / WORKSPACE

POD / SEAT ASSEMBLIES

EXPANSION MECHANISMS
    ↕

    ↕

Construction order:

Product Law

→ verification

Never reverse this order by forcing new semantics into old prototype geometry.

### Visual-reference interpretation

The project Library activation storyboard is treated as a **mechanical/visual reference**, not a product-number or timing authority. It depicts eight inner pods in its reference composition and four specialized outer mechanisms, while the canonical 029 machine supports up to ten durable/presentation Seat slots. Therefore the reference may guide silhouette, material, energy, hologram, and choreography language, but must not override the 1–10 Seat capacity or invent permanent geometry from storyboard coordinates. Its 00:00–00:10 panel labels are not runtime timing law because no video asset is available. Specialized outer mechanism semantics are an S7 construction responsibility and must map to real product/facility meaning before final rendering.

### Root inheritance law

**S0-S10 are the canonical structural roots of the 029 spatial machine, not merely the first ten implementation phases.** Once established, their contracts are inherited by every later slice S11-S33.

Every later slice MUST consume and preserve the applicable S0-S10 roots for semantic identity and payload ownership, physical assembly ownership, Pod/Seat and Division relationships, expansion and articulation, facility placement, ports/corridors/semantic edges/clearance, energy and signal projection, semantic camera subjects and geometry-aware framing, accessibility, responsive behavior, reduced motion, and verification seams.

Later slices may extend these roots with product capabilities, runtime projections, or world expression, but may not create a parallel spatial grammar, duplicate authority, invent alternate identities, or redefine earlier machine contracts for convenience.

This means **Workspace (S13), Team/Agents (S14), MCP/Capability (S15), budget/handoff (S16), and Storage/Commerce/Settings (S18-S20), plus every other product feature or facility, must be physically and semantically realized through the machine established by S0-S10.** A later feature is not allowed to become an isolated UI island or an invented Seat child merely because it appears later in the feature sequence.

### World-expression inheritance law

**S22-S29 are cross-cutting world-expression and interaction layers over the S0-S21 machine.** They are not a separate visual architecture and they do not replace the structural roots.

Accessibility, responsive behavior, materials, lighting, holograms, ambient environment, performance, cross-feature choreography, and final interaction choreography must operate on the same semantic machine, topology, geometry, and camera subjects established by the earlier slices. When those concerns expose a structural constraint, the implementation resolves it at the owning root rather than forking the model in S22-S29.

### Theme inheritance law

The light and dark spatial expressions derive from the **single canonical unified theme root** owned by Product Law:

- **Light Spatial Skeuomorphism** = Instrument Space expression.
- **Dark Spatial Glassmorphism** = Command Space expression.

S22-S29 may express and refine these modes inside the 3D world, but they must not create a second theme authority or allow theme mode to change identity, authorization, durable state, scheduler behavior, commerce truth, or other backend-owned meaning.

The result is an inheritance chain, not three independent programs:

`Product Law / canonical theme root → S0-S10 structural roots → S11-S21 product/runtime realization → S22-S29 world expression → S30-S33 proof, acceptance, and polish.`

## 2. Physical machine grammar

The finished world is not a generic tree rendered in WebGL. Semantic trees may remain useful for product hierarchy, but physical composition is organized as assemblies.

WORLD
├── environment / deep-space field
├── central machine
├── pod ring
├── facility ring
├── connection corridors
├── camera / navigation
├── presentation effects
└── accessibility / responsive projection

### Central core

The core is the shared Workspace operational center and must become a layered mechanical assembly, not an empty disc.

Required concepts:

- structural outer shell
- receiving surface
- internal core / reactor chamber
- concentric mechanisms
- semantic ports
- contribution intake/outflow
- workspace payload surface
- status / turn presentation
- receiving / absorb / reflect / handoff choreography
- stable center camera subject

### Pod assemblies

The active machine capacity is up to ten Seats.

A Pod is a physical projection of a Seat, not a durable Seat itself.

POD

├── structural collar
├── internal chamber
├── rotating / articulated mechanism
├── semantic payload surface
